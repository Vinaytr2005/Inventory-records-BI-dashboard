# 📊 Inventory Records BI Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** developed to analyze inventory and product performance data. The dashboard provides a visual overview of product costs, units sold, and product-wise performance to make inventory data easier to understand and analyze.

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Inventory Records BI Dashboard** transforms inventory records into an interactive visual report. It focuses on comparing products based on their **cost price, units sold, and overall product performance**.

The dashboard includes separate visualizations for different product groups, including wireless gadgets and gaming products.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze product-wise inventory and sales records.
* Compare the number of units sold across products.
* Identify products with higher cost contributions.
* Understand the distribution of units sold across product categories.
* Present inventory data through an interactive and easy-to-understand dashboard.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### Inventory & Product Analysis

* Product-wise **total cost price** analysis.
* Product-wise **units sold** comparison.
* Cost analysis based on the number of units sold.
* Product performance comparison using interactive charts.
* KPI cards for selected product groups.
* Visual breakdown of wireless gadget and gaming product data.

### Key Visualizations

* 📊 Product-wise Cost Price
* 🍩 Units Sold by Product
* 📈 Cost Price vs. Units Sold
* 📊 Gaming Product Cost Comparison
* 🔢 KPI Cards
* 📋 Product Performance Analysis
